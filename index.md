## About AgileFlow

AgileFlow helps users understand and apply Agile and Scrum accurately while distinguishing:

- Formal Scrum requirements
- Agile values and principles
- Common Agile practices
- Optional techniques
- Course or academic requirements
- Team-specific conventions
- Information that is not established by the available sources

The Playbook is designed to support transparent, context-aware Agile/Scrum guidance rather than prescribing one universal Agile process.
